📋 Checklist Card — AVCO Lycoming O-320

Pre-Start (back of card)

  1. Secure test cell with tiedown lugs & chains.
  2. Inspect engine, prop, hardware & plumbing for loose/broken items.
  3. Oil quantity 6 qt (Aeroshell 15W-50). Secure cap.
  4. All switches OFF (down).
  5. All circuit breakers IN.
  6. Push-pull controls: Throttle closed · Mixture idle cut-off · Fuel shut-off closed (all pulled toward operator).
  7. Attach external power (APU) if desired.
  8. Check fuel level.
  9. Drain fuel strainer; inspect for water/contamination; keep draining until clear.

Engine Start (front)

  1. Clear area. Stand clear of propeller!
  2. Push in Fuel Shut-off Valve.
  3. Master switch ON.
  4. Fuel pump switch ON.
  5. Mixture forward — FULL RICH.
  6. Throttle full in once, then back out.
  7. Throttle ¼–½ inch from rear stop.
  8. Both magneto switches ON.
  9. Verify prop area clear of all obstructions.
  10. Engage start switch. (No start → repeat 4-10.)
  11. Alternator switch ON.
  12. Warm up — oil-temp needle off low stop before exceeding 1500 RPM.
  13. Do not exceed 2400 RPM.

✍️ Handwritten: “Go to 1100 RPM — do magneto check.”

Engine Shut Down (front)

  1. Idle 1 minute.
  2. Mixture to idle cutoff — engine stops within 10 s.
  3. Both mags OFF (after engine has stopped).
  4. Master, alternator, fuel pump OFF.
  5. Pull out Fuel Shut-off Valve.

Operating Limitations & Ranges

  • Tach: idle 600 · max 2,500 RPM
  • Fuel pressure 1–7 psi
  • Oil pressure 35 psi @ idle · 85 psi @ maximum
  • Oil temp normal 165–230 °F
  • Voltage 12–14 ± 0.5
